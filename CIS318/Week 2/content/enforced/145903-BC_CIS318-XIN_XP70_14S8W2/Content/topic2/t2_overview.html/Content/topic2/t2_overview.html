Topic 2 . Ethical Behavior of IT Professionals and Users

Topic Overview

We begin this topic with a discussion of whether IT workers are professionals under the legal definition of professional workers. The topic provides an overview of professional relationships between IT professionals and their clients, suppliers, other IT professionals, and users. We will explore ethical issues relevant to these relationships. The topic will introduce you to the codes of ethics of various professional organizations, such as the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) and the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM). We will discuss ethical issues unique to IT professionals, such as access to information, use of computing resources, and software piracy.

Learning Outcomes/Objectives

At the end of this topic, you will be able to:

  • Compare the different codes of ethics for information technology (IT) professionals.
  • Define the common ethical issues that must be managed or faced by IT professionals.
  • Analyze the IT user approaches to support ethical practices.
  • Compile a list of three IT-related ethical codes and discuss why it is important to have such codes for IT professionals
