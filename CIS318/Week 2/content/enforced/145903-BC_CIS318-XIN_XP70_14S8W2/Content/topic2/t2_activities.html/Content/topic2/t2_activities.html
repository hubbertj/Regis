Are There Computer, Technology, and Internet Ethics? Video Discussion

Context: Information technology professionals and the myriad of technologies they work and interact with daily can have a profound effect on businesses and employees. Unethical behavior by IT professionals could be catastrophic, causing loss of customers, reduced revenues, and government scrutiny.

Ethical behavior is important in business. Consider the savings and loan fiasco, the Enron disaster, and the subprime mortgage banking crisis, to name just a few. Looking behind the scenes of business, we see a complex and ever-changing condition. Employees, customers, suppliers, and others interact to “make” the business what it is – or is not. Of critical importance is the IT professional. IT professionals have access to a myriad of information, much of which is sensitive in nature. Social Security numbers, dates of birth, medical information, banking information, etc. are all within easy reach. Unethical behavior by any one of a business’s IT professionals could spell disaster for hundreds or thousands of people, on top of the blow to the reputation of the business itself.

The video, delivered by blogger Chris Pirillo, discusses several ethical issues related to computing. Chris provides a definition of ethics, discusses enforcement of ethical behavior on the web, and discusses the need for ethical codes in computing. While lighthearted, Chris is serious about this important topic.

Reviewing this video will help you understand the importance of ethical behavior in computing.

Task Description: Review the video then discuss the following questions in the Discussion Forum.

Are There Computer, Techology and Internet Ethics?
[key word search: Pirillo, ethics, Internet, technology, code-of-conduct, ethical behavior]

  1. What do you think about Chris’s definition of ethics? Is he correct or incorrect? Did he leave something out of his definition?
  2. We heard Chris’s definition of computer ethics; what is your definition? Explain why you think your definition is correct.
  3. Chris said he lives by a code of ethics wherein he subscribes to what he called “The Golden Rule” of doing unto others. Chris also said he trusts others inherently until that trust is broken. Explain your personal code of ethics, and tell us why your code is a valid one to have for an IT professional.

Case Study Discussion

Context: Information Technology (IT) professionals have incredible access to unimaginable amounts of confidential information, both at the corporate- and personal-levels. The access of IT professionals to systems, files, reports, etc requires a level of trust by their employers and sterling ethical standards from the IT employees. The following case study highlights such standards and the failure of upper management to reward such high ethical standards. Understanding that taking the moral high ground can have adverse consequences is important for our study of ethics.

Task Description: Read the case study provided below and answer the following questions. Post your answers as an initial response.

Topic 2 Case Study

  1. What message is sent to IT workers by the actions of New York Law School and Collegis— even if unrelated job-performance issues justified their actions in firing Gross and Perry?
  2. Since this incident, a number of states have enacted laws that require workers to report immediately any child pornography found while servicing equipment. Most of the laws state that a worker who reports such a discovery is immune from any criminal, civil, or administrative liability. Failure to report the discovery can result in a fine, imprisonment, or both. Do you think such laws will encourage reporting? Why or why not?
