Here is optional additional material provided for your reference. You may want to use this material to reinforce your discussions in the Discussion Forum or to support your written assignments throughout the course.

http://ethics.iit.edu/index1.php/Programs/Codes%20of%20Ethics

http://www.ibe.org.uk/codesofethics/codesofethics.html

Here is another link on creating a code of ethics from Dr. Chris MacDonald. Start with the three links at the top of the page. There are more links to other material you can read and use as well.

http://www.ethicsweb.ca/codes/

This link takes you to The Ten Commandments of Computer Ethics from the Computer Ethics Institute. These ten items are an excellent guide for IT professionals to use and abide by.

http://cpsr.org/issues/ethics/cei/
