Ethical Behavior of IT Professionals

There are many different codes of ethics for the IT profession. Below are links and articles concerning ethical codes. Read the material and think about the underlying similarities between the codes.

The following article will provide you with a broad understanding of computer and information ethics. The Other Internet Resources section of the article contains several excellent links to other documents and websites:

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Computer and Information Ethics

http://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-computer/

Read the following article to gain a better understanding of the need for ethical codes:

Ethical Standards for Information Systems Professionals: A Case for a Unified Code

Ethical Behavior of IT Users

As an IT professional, there are codes of ethics for the work that is done in support of the profession. Research indicates there are very few end-user codes of ethics. Some laws and legal codes exist that provide rules for users. For example, the prohibition against copying (pirating) software a user has not purchased. However, a comprehensive end-user code of ethics is hard to find. Below are several articles that present how technology is used or abused by end users.

Read the following articles to gain a better understanding of how technology is used or abused by users.

Invitation to a Public Debate on Ethical Computer Use

Creation and Implementation of Policy for Information Technology Use: Abounding Ethics and Values Issues

Think About This

How can IT professionals build systems that minimize end-user abuse? Is this even possible?
