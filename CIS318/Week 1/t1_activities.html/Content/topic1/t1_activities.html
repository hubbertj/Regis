Ethical Theory and Philosophy in Business Ethics Video Discussion

Context: Reviewing ethical theories is an excellent way to begin this week. The video discusses several different ethical theories, why ethical theories are needed, what is the most effective way to introduce theory into a business ethics course, how ethical theory and philosophy are relevant to today’s managers, and how ethical theory can be useful in guiding a practicing manager through a difficult decision-making process.

Reviewing this video will help you understand the importance of ethical theories as a basis to the material you will be studying during this course.

Task Description: Review the video then discuss the following questions in the Discussion Forum.

Ethical Theory and Philosophy in Business Ethics

[key word search: ethics, ethical theory, business ethics, Norman Bowie, managerial decision-making, Kant, business and philosophy, Kantian ethics]

  1. Based on the video, what do you think is the correlation between ethics and business?
  2. In the video, Dr. Bowie says:

    If you don’t realize as a manager that people are coming from different ethical perspectives, then you won’t understand some of the conflict that goes on in an organization. You won’t understand why some of your directives aren’t obeyed, because you may be operating as a utilitarian, one of your employees might be a Kantian, and you just never get what it’s all about. (Business Roundtable Institute for Corporate Ethics, 2005)

    Do you think it is important as a manager or leader in business to understand ethical theories as a means to understand better the perspectives of your employees?
  3. Do we even need ethics in business? Is not doing what is best for the shareholders and the company more important? What is your opinion?

Delivery: Your active participation in the Discussion Forum is expected. To complete this assignment, post your answers to the above questions by midnight (MT) Wednesday of the week the assignment is due. Then on Thursday, Friday, and/or Saturday, provide substantial comments on at least two other student postings. (“Nice Job” is not considered a substantial comment.)

Case Study Discussion

Context: By definition, "ethics is a set of beliefs about right and wrong behavior within a society" (Reynolds, 2010, p. 3). Ethics is an important issue for businesses. Failure to behave ethically can have adverse affects on a company’s bottom line and damage its reputation. The following case study demonstrates two ethical issues. The first issue concerns leaking of confidential information to the press. The second issue concerns the use of unethical methods to uncover the perpetrator. Making oneself aware of such ethical issues is an important step in combating unethical behavior.

Task Description: Read the case study provided below and answer the following questions. Post your answers as an initial response.

Topic 1 Case Study

  1. Which issue is more disconcerting—the fact that a board member leaked confidential information about the firm or the tactics used to investigate the leak? Defend your position.
  2. Can the use of pretexting to gain information ever be justified? Is it considered legal under any circumstances?

Delivery: Post your initial response in the Forum by midnight (MT) Wednesday. Post your two replies, at a minimum, by midnight (MT) Saturday.

Discussion Forum Rubric

Written Assignment

Context: You are the newly appointed manager of a high-tech company that is suffering serious financial problems. Corporate headquarters is planning layoffs, which you know will affect your department. An employee you supervise, who used to be a coworker prior to your promotion, has approached you with the following question: “Do you know if there will be layoffs in our group? I’ve got a mortgage and a kid in college. A layoff would be catastrophic to me at this time.”

Task Description: Research three ethical theories/approaches, such as utilitarianism (teleology), relativism, deontology, egalitarianism, etc. Write a brief paragraph on each theory/approach. In this paragraph, provide a definition of the theory/approach, cite pros and cons, and state why you chose the theory/approach. Next, select one of the three theories/approaches. Use this theory/approach to frame your response to the employee in the scenario above. Be sure to provide the rationale for your response.

Delivery: Deliver your research, response, and rationale in a paper of 2-3 pages. Content, quality of writing, use of APA formatting, use of in-text citations from your resources, and effective writing are all elements of your grade. To complete this assignment, post your paper to the Drop Box by midnight (MT) Saturday of the week the assignment is due.
