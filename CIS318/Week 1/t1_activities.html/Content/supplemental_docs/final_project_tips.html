Final Project Notes & Tips

Final Project Notes

Students who have taken this course in the past sometimes have found the final project difficult, so here are several tips that should help you out.

  1. Plan to structure the paper as a normal or typical paper you would write in any other college course. You need an introduction, a body, and a conclusion, and you must move between sections using transition paragraphs. Your reader must be able to read your paper easily from start to finish as it flows from paragraph to paragraph, section to section, covering all of the requirements.
  2. Please include a title page and a reference list.
  3. Type the paper double-spaced with 1-inch margins all around. Please remember to use headings throughout your paper.
  4. Use the outline in the Project Description to guide your thoughts. This is an outline, so follow it.
  5. Please pay particular attention to the following:
    1. Do not put the paper together into three separate sections. Write the paper as one continuous work.
    2. Start out with a simple opening stating that you are starting a new company that will be involved in such-and-such industry.
    3. It is important to address ethical issues from day one to set the tone for your company; consequently, you need to specify why you picked XYZ as the name of the company.
    4. Within the body, you will have your code of ethics and an explanation of why you chose that code. If you choose to use a code from another organization, such as IEEE, do not include that document. Instead, zero in on critical parts of the code and provide your explanations and reasoning from there.
    5. Include explanations/reasoning regarding why you made the choices you did.
    6. The first part of your paper explains your code of ethics and other documents, and why you did what you did. Include your actual document – weave it into your text.
    7. Do not use a Question/Answer, Question/Answer, etc. format.
    8. The second part of your paper should have a transition paragraph stating the other types of policies, forms, contracts, etc. that you have. This may be a listing with a brief description of what the contents would be as well as why they are ethical to have in your new company. You do not have to create these documents. Remember, there are no rules in writing academic papers that preclude you from including lists in your paper.
    9. The third part of the paper may be more difficult to approach. Therefore, you can introduce the required situation into your paper as a case study. Within this case study, draw upon the documents you have identified earlier in your paper to arrive at your solution.

It helps if you look at real-world company ethical codes before you start your project. The web offers many examples. In addition, you can use the resources provided in this course.

Final Project Tips

Cite all direct quotes and paraphrases. If you use an idea, such as a part of an existing code, then you have to cite your source(s). Follow APA rules.

In writing your paper, do not write something like this:

Part 1 – You are starting your own Information Systems/Technology company. You are going to create the major framework for your company’s business practices, policies ….

  1. Company Name: 

  1. Why is the name ethical to use? Answer answer answer.

  2. Company Logo: 

  1. Why is the logo ethical to use? Answer answer answer.

  3. Company Mission: 

  1. Why is the mission ethical? Answer answer answer.

Submitting a paper in this format will not earn a high grade.

The paper must flow between the parts. Use transitions. Use section headings and paragraphs. It is a good idea to use subheadings. Include introductions and conclusions. Avoid the numbering format as shown above. Do not repeat the text from the Project Description as shown above.

Note: Headings and subheadings are optional. You do not have to use them. However, using these elements in your paper will help you to meet the requirements stated under the Organization component of the Effective Writing part of the Final Project Rubric.

Look at the assignment as you would any other college-level paper, and use the Project Description as a guide to writing your paper. You could structure your paper as follows. (Note: Other formats and approaches are fine. You do not have to use the following format.)

Introduction

Company Name

Explain why you chose this name, your rationale, and why this name is ethical to use.

XYZ Corporation Logo

Explain why you chose this logo, your rationale, and why this logo is ethical to use. You may insert a drawing, picture, or image of your logo in this section.

XYZ Corporation Mission

Provide your mission statement here. Explain why you chose this mission statement and why this statement is ethical.

XYZ Corporation Product(s) (or) Service(s)

Provide a short description of your product(s) and/or service(s) here. Explain why the product(s) and/or service(s) are ethical.

XYZ Corporation Code of Ethics

Your code of ethics goes here. If you use your own code, you do not have to go into fine details. In a short paragraph, state each part of your code (see below for examples of some parts you might want to include). 

If you use an existing code, state which code you used and why. Then select what you think are the important parts of that code. As stated above, in a short paragraph, state each part of the code.

Regardless of which approach you take, this is a great place for you to use subheadings. Subheadings will allow you to separate clearly each part of your code.

For example:

Work with Integrity
Protection of Personal Information
Protection of Confidentiality and Intellectual Information
Respect for Copyright Laws
Bribery
Violations of the XYZ Corporation Code of Ethics

Following your description of the parts, now discuss how these parts work together to foster and sustain your company’s ethical standards. For example, you could use this sub-heading:

XYZ Corporation Code of Ethics: Discussion

XYZ Corporation Ethics Training Program

In paragraph format, answer the questions from the Project Description here.

XYZ Corporation Grievance Policy

Write what your grievance policy is here. (Note: You can use the Internet to find such policies. You can “cherry pick” parts of these policies to fit your needs. Do not forget to cite.) Now state why this policy is ethical.

XYZ Corporation Code of Ethics Supporting Documents

This is Part 2 from the Project Description. This is another great place to use subheadings. In paragraph format, state what each document, policy, form, etc. is and provide an explanation of why this item is included and why it is ethical.

For example:

Acceptable Use Policy

XYZ Corporation will have an acceptable use policy for its company network. The policy will include these major sections:

  1. How we monitor email communications:  This section will explain that for security reasons and in order to protect company confidential information, XYZ Corporation may monitor and store email.  Employees should not assume email sent on the company networks is private.
  2. Use of company resources:
  3. Etc.

    Explanation of why included and why ethical could go here.

    4. Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement

As you can see, you do not have to go into minute detail. Simple, direct statements work best.

Case Study: Implementation of the XYZ Corporation Code of Ethics

This is Part 3 from the Project Description. For example, you could start this section as follows:

As a means to review and discuss how XYZ Corporation’s code of ethics and supporting documents would be implemented should there be a possible violation of the code of ethics, the following case study is presented.

The case is that of a senior technical employee leaving XYZ Corporation to work for a major competitor.  Etc., etc.

Of course, there are other ways to handle this. Be creative. Ensure you present the situation as stated in the Project Description. Then, using your code and other documents, describe and discuss what actions you would take. Use the Project Description to guide your thoughts.

Conclusion

Wrap up your paper. Tie everything together in a couple of paragraphs.
