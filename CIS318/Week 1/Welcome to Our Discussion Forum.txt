Ethical Theory and Philosophy in Business Ethics Video Discussion

Context: Reviewing ethical theories is an excellent way to begin this week. The video discusses several different ethical theories, why ethical theories are needed, what is the most effective way to introduce theory into a business ethics course, how ethical theory and philosophy are relevant to today’s managers, and how ethical theory can be useful in guiding a practicing manager through a difficult decision-making process.

Reviewing this video will help you understand the importance of ethical theories as a basis to the material you will be studying during this course.

    If you don’t realize as a manager that people are coming from different ethical perspectives, then you won’t understand some of the conflict that goes on in an organization. (Business Roundtable Institute for Corporate Ethics, 2005)
