While this article is not specifically about trust, the article deals with the creation of trust. Ethical and unethical actions by an individual or business create trust or distrust respectively. When we surf the web, we may only visit sites that are rated “secure” by our virus protection software, such as that done through McAfee’s use of a checkmark next to tested web sites. Trust is an important topic for companies since consumers who are concerned about their privacy may only do business with companies that provide secure web environments.
