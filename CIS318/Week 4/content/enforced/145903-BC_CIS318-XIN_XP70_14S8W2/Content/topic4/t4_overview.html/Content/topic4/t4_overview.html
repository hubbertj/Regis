Topic 4 . Freedom of Expression and Intellectual Property

Topic Overview

This week’s topic presents a discussion concerning freedom of expression and intellectual property. There are laws in support of both of these areas, but as with most laws, they do not provide hard and fast rules that can be followed in every situation. Many ethical decisions must be made personally and professionally when dealing with information systems and allowing for too much expression and what constitutes intellectual property.

Learning Outcomes/Objectives

At the end of this topic, you will be able to:

  • Identify the legal basis for freedom of expression and illustrate how the Internet has an impact on this area.
  • Review several United States government freedom of expression laws and acts in the areas of defamation, pornography, blogging, obscene speech, and freedom of speech on the Internet.
  • Discuss the relationship between employee freedom of expression and business concerns regarding confidentiality and IT system use.
  • Explain the term intellectual property, and discuss means to protect intellectual property on IT systems.
  • Discuss why organizations are concerned about protecting their intellectual property, and describe the role of information technology in both the exploitation and protection of intellectual property.
