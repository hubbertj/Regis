Intellectual Property Video Discussion

Context: As we learned from our additional readings for this topic, intellectual property, according to Reynolds (2010), “is a term used to describe works of the mind – such as art, books, films, formulas, inventions, music, and processes – that are distinct, and owned or created by a single person or group” (p. 197). Intellectual property concerns copyrights, patents, and trade secrets, all of which are protected under various U.S. and international laws.

In the video, Mr. J. Craig Williams focuses on problems U.S. firms face regarding copyright infringement. Mr. Williams mentions the Digital Millennium Copyright Act as an effort by the U.S. Congress to address this issue. Mr. Williams suggests that a combination of increased enforcement of existing copyright laws, coupled with changes to these laws, is required to address the increased difficulty of enforcement presented by the Internet.

Reviewing this video will help you understand the importance of protection of intellectual property--of which copyrights are one aspect--by business as an apparent crucial element of corporate survival.

Case Study Discussion

Context: We have two case studies in Topic 4, one on freedom of expression and the other on intellectual property. The facilitator will split the class in half and assign each half of the class one of the two case studies.

Freedom of expression. The term resounds in what we as Americans value most. However, is there a limit where such freedom must be constrained? The following case study demonstrates that not only can such activities be illegal, they can also be deadly. It is important to understand the role of ethics in the use of IT-based systems, such as cell phones and computers. Such an understanding should spur businesses to create and enforce computer usage policies.

Various laws protect intellectual property, such as books, music, patents, etc. Users of IT systems, IT professionals, and hackers all can use such systems to steal intellectual property. For college students, plagiarism is of utmost importance. The following case study looks into theft of music using IT-based systems. The loss of revenue is staggering, with legal entanglements a very real prospect for thieves. Understanding the circumstances surrounding intellectual property, as well as the potential costs for violation of intellectual property, is an important lesson for businesses to grasp, leading to inclusion of the topic of intellectual property within a businesses’ ethics policy.

Task Description: Read both case studies provided below. Answer the two questions for your assigned case study. Post your answers as an initial response.

Case Study #1: Sexting

Topic 4 Sexting Case Study

  1. Does sexting represent a form of expression that is protected by the First Amendment?
  2. What can be done to protect people from the dangers of sexting while still safeguarding our First Amendment rights?

Topic 4 RIAA Music Piracy Case Study

  1. Is the RIAA’s strong stand on copyright infringement helping or hurting the music recording industry?
  2. Could an ISP’s implementation and enforcement of the RIAA’s multitier strategy have a negative impact on the ISP?
