Part 2: Forum Activities

Discussion Question(s)

Please post your response to the following questions in the Forum by Wednesday midnight, of Week 2. Respond substantively to at least two other postings by fellow students.

"Substantively" means a thoughtful, critical response to the ideas in the posting by engaging the material presented.  Your responses could include, for example, appreciation of the posting and why; agreement with specific comments; a difference of opinion with justifications; corrections of inaccuracies with specific references; or questions the posting may provoke. A "substantive" posting is not "I agree with what you said. Great job."

Forum Postings: Please respond to all of the following:

  1. Discuss three characteristics of Sanatana Dharma which have resulted in an inclusionary worldview, so that in Hinduism there is a range of worship from polytheism to monotheism and nontheism.
  2. Explain the Hindu understanding of Brahman, atman, and their relationship to moksha.
  3. Discuss the caste system including: how it is validated in the sacred texts, the dharma of each caste and the current situation. What impact did Mahatma Gandhi have?
  4. View one of the following videos and give a succinct report: "Water" directed by Deepa Mehta; "Monsoon Wedding" directed by Mira Nair; "Gandhi" directed by Richard Attenborough; The "River" directed by Jean Renoir.
