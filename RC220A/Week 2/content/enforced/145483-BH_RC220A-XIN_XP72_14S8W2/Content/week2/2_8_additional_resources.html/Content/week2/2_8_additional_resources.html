2.8 Additional Resources

Online Resources

Further online resources on Sanatana Dharma:

  • Hinduism: From the University of Wyoming, the best, most comprehensive link for eastern and western religious traditions.
  • Hinduism and select Hinduism from the pull-down menu:

Living Traditions' companion web site features self-evaluative quizzes, resources, including videos, research tools and other online activities. Be sure to visit their Destinations link for more online resources.

  • Mahatma Gandhi: This is a Mahatma Gandhi website, and has some great resources by and on Gandhi.
  • Siddhartha Gautama: From Shippensberg University, a narrative of the Buddha's life with some images.
  • Siddhartha Gautama: From Washington State University, a nicely presented overview of the Buddha's life and principles of Buddhism.
  • The Buddha's Life: From the National Capital of Canada, one of the first sites on Buddhism on the web.
  • Indian History Sourcebook: Sources on the Buddha's Life and Death: From Fordham University, this a great place to find primary resources on India and Hinduism.
