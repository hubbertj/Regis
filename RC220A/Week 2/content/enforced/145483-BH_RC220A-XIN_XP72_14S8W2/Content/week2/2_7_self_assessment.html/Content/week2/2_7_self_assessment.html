2.7 Self-Assessment Questions

Answering the following questions will help you engage with the reading material and activities in depth. This activity will also help focus and enrich your Forum postings and essays.

  1. Can the Vedas be described as reflecting a nature-worship religion?
  2. In what ways do the Upanishads differ from and agree with earlier Vedas?
  3. What is the relationship between the gods: Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva ?
  4. What is the Hindu view of women? Compare it to our social view of gender, remembering that women in the US only got the vote in 1920 and the Equal Rights Amendment for Women was voted down in the 1970's.
  5. Why is our normal, daily life called "samsara?"
  6. What is moksha? Why is it desirable and how does one achieve it?
