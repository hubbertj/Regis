Technology Tools

You will begin using VoiceThread for one of the discussions in Topic 3, Activity 2. Your facilitator will provide you with login and Group information. Note: Each VoiceThread section of this course contains its own private discussion area. If you need assistance, check with your facilitator immediately.

VoiceThread – Conversations in the Cloud, http://voicethread.com/. This course uses the free web-based VoiceThread tool for many of the discussions throughout the course. It is a fun and easy to use multimedia tool that you can use to share files and/or simply provide a “voice” to your discussion responses.

  • VoiceThread Introduction
  • Tutorials--There are many, many tutorials in VoiceThread for you to get started right away. The “What’s a VoiceThread anyway?” VoiceThread video tutorial, which is available without logging in by selecting the Browse tab at the top of the VoiceThread homepage.
  • Equipment needed ($0-$15):
  • You will need to use a headset and microphone though some computers include both of these internally.
  • A webcam is not required. You may upload a still shot photo of yourself or simply use the default icon provided by VoiceThread (though this is not recommended since the icons are impersonal).

Note: If you encounter any technical difficulties, contact your facilitator immediately.
