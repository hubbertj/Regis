Course Description

Conveys a sense of what philosophy has been and engages students in a philosophical reflection on perennial and contemporary issues. Emphasizes knowledge, freedom, value and society.

(top)

Course Prerequisites

None

(top)

Course Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, learners should be able to:

  1. Identify the major issues in philosophical discourse and the positions of individual philosophers on these issues.
  2. Compare and contrast the positions of philosophers on major issues.
  3. Construct philosophically sound arguments that articulate your own position on these major issues.
  4. Discuss Jesuit values as they relate to the subject matter.
(top)
